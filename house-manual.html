House manual

Welcome to Glashaus. A few things to know so your stay goes smoothly.

Coming and going

  • Text us at (209) 559-4966 when you arrive and when you leave.
  • Maximum 2 guests. No pets, no events.
  • A camera at the gate records incoming traffic.

In the dome

  • To open the curtains, push them in toward the dome and slide slowly along the track.
  • In summer, close the curtains when you're out to help keep the dome cool.
  • Remotes are labeled.
  • Please keep blankets inside, and don't move the furniture. Only the lights may go outside.

Fire

  • No wood fires or bonfires. The propane fire table is yours to use; instructions are on the table and propane is provided.
  • Indoor gas fireplace: in winter it runs by remote, and the pilot light stays on even when the fire is off. In summer it is switched off so the pilot doesn't heat the dome.

Outside

  • The terrain is uneven. Watch your step, especially after dark.
  • There are wild animals and poison oak.
  • Outdoor solar lights are automatic. Please don't switch them off.
  • Put all garbage in the large bins outside and close the lids tightly.

Please

  • No smoking or vaping, inside or out.
  • No loud music after 9 PM, and please respect the neighbors.
  • Don't turn off any systems (gas or water).
  • Leave the property as you found it, and tell us if anything is missing or not working.

The fine print

Use of the property is at your own risk, and you agree to hold the owners harmless for any injury or damage. We may cancel a stay for rule violations, including complaints from neighbors.

If we can make your experience better, please let us know. Have an amazing time!
